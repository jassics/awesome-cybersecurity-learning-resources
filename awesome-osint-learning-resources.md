# Awesome OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence) Learning Resources

OSINT is the practice of collecting and analyzing publicly-available information to support investigations: recon for red team / bug bounty, threat intelligence, fraud / brand-protection, due-diligence, law-enforcement, and journalism. Learn the methodology first (tasking, collection, analysis, reporting, OPSEC) and the tools follow naturally.
