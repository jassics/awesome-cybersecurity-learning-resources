# Awesome Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) Learning Resources

Cyber Threat Intelligence covers strategic, operational, tactical, and technical intelligence. It's the discipline of collecting, analyzing, and disseminating information about adversaries, their TTPs (Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures), infrastructure, and motivations so defenders can act on it. Standard frameworks: MITRE ATT&CK, the Cyber Kill Chain, the Diamond Model, and F3EAD.

## Books
1. [Intelligence-Driven Incident Response, 2nd Ed by Scott J. Roberts, Rebekah Brown (O'Reilly, 2023)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/intelligence-driven-incident-response/9781098120672/) - Canonical CTI + IR integration book.
2. [Practical Threat Intelligence and Data-Driven Threat Hunting by Valentina Costa-Gazcon (Packt)](https://www.packtpub.com/product/practical-threat-intelligence-and-data-driven-threat-hunting/9781838556372)
3. [Psychology of Intelligence Analysis by Richards J. Heuer Jr. (CIA, free)](https://www.cia.gov/static/9a5f1162fd0932c29bfed1c030edf4ae/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf) - Required reading; covers cognitive biases.
4. [Structured Analytic Techniques for Intelligence Analysis by Heuer, Pherson](https://sk.sagepub.com/books/structured-analytic-techniques-for-intelligence-analysis-3e) - Practical analytic method reference.
5. [The Threat Intelligence Handbook by Recorded Future (free)](https://www.recordedfuture.com/resources/the-threat-intelligence-handbook)
6. [Visual Threat Intelligence by Thomas Roccia (free)](https://www.securitybreak.io/visual-threat-intelligence)
7. [Operator Handbook: Red Team + OSINT + Blue Team Reference by Joshua Picolet](https://www.amazon.com/Operator-Handbook-Red-OSINT-Reference/dp/B08RRDTNDV)
8. [The Cuckoo's Egg by Clifford Stoll](https://www.amazon.com/Cuckoos-Egg-Tracking-Computer-Espionage/dp/1416507787) - Classic CTI origin story.
9. [Countdown to Zero Day by Kim Zetter](https://www.amazon.com/Countdown-Zero-Day-Stuxnet-Digital/dp/0770436196) - Stuxnet deep-dive, still highly relevant.
